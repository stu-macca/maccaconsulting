About Me

Learn More About My Mission

I am a sales and tendering consultant who has helped businesses pursue their revenue growth objectives through effective tendering processes and win strategies since 2000. I provide flexible consulting support, from focused strategic assignments and major tenders through to ongoing embedded tender capability. I started my career with Toyota New Zealand, where I first learned how to engineer and apply robust, right-sized processes. My tendering experience began with a start-up in the United Kingdom and grew through roles in Australia with global organisations including General Electric, Alstom and Landis+Gyr. Today, I combine that experience with practical use of AI and automation to help clients work more efficiently without compromising commercial judgement or submission quality.
