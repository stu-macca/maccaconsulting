BEFORE YOU GET IN TOUCH

A few useful answers.

What photos should I send?

A wide view of the whole tree or stump, a closer view of the concern, and a photo of the gate or side access. Include nearby buildings and fences. Take photos from a safe position.

Can I request more than one service?

Yes. Choose the main job in the form and describe any additional tree or stump work in the job details. The final service list is still to be confirmed.

Are quotes free?

The design uses a free-quote call to action from the brief. Phil’s quote policy, written-quote process and any site-assessment fees must be confirmed before launch.

Do you offer emergency tree work?

Emergency and after-hours availability has not been confirmed. This prototype does not take urgent requests or provide an emergency response service.

Will this form send an enquiry?

This is a client-review prototype. You can try the complete form and photo previews, but nothing is sent to Phil. Details and photos stay in this page and are cleared when you reload.
