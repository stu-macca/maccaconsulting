HOLLOW CREATION

Start with the tree and the habitat.

Tree hollows can provide shelter and nesting places for wildlife. Share a wide view of the tree, any existing hollows, and the surrounding garden or property so you can discuss the project with Ben.

The right approach depends on the tree and the site. Ben’s hollow-creation methods, assessment process, species considerations and ongoing maintenance offer are still to be confirmed. A photo enquiry is a starting point, not confirmation that a tree is suitable.

BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

What to include.

  • A wide photo of the whole tree and its surroundings, taken from ground level.
  • Any existing hollows or habitat features visible from a safe position.
  • Your habitat goals and any wildlife you have observed.
  • Your suburb, property access and nearby buildings or fences.

Service area, insurance, qualifications and business hours are still to be supplied. Photos help describe the job; they do not replace a site assessment where one is needed.
