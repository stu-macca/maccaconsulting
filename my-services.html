Whether initiated well in advance or at the beginning of bid preparation, my Win Strategy Process provides significant value. AI can accelerate tender production, but customer understanding, competitive positioning and the decisions behind a winning strategy still require experienced commercial judgement.

My Approach

My Approach

AI, Confidentiality and Quality

AI can improve the speed and consistency of tender work, but it must be used with appropriate controls. The approach for each engagement is agreed with the client, taking account of their policies, approved systems and the sensitivity of the information involved.

Will AI write my tender?

AI may assist with activities such as analysing documents, organising information, developing initial drafts, comparing requirements and improving consistency. It does not replace tender strategy, professional judgement or client decision-making.

How do you protect confidential information?

Before using AI, we agree what information may be processed and which tools may be used. Information is minimised, redacted or de-identified where appropriate. Confidential, commercially sensitive or personal information is not entered into public AI tools without suitable controls and client approval.

Which AI tools do you use?

Only tools and use cases appropriate to the engagement are used. Where required, this includes reviewing the provider's data-handling, retention, model-training, access and third-party integration settings. If an acceptable AI environment is unavailable, the work is completed without AI or using suitably de-identified information.

How is AI-generated work verified?

AI output is treated as working material until it has been reviewed. Important facts, dates, claims, credentials, evidence and compliance requirements are checked against the tender documents and authorised client information. Questions or discrepancies are referred to the client rather than resolved through unsupported assumptions.

Who makes the final decisions?

People do. AI does not make final decisions about whether to bid, pricing, commercial or contractual commitments, win strategy or submission approval. These decisions remain subject to experienced human judgement and client authorisation.

Can you work within our AI and information-security policies?

Yes. Any relevant client policies, data classifications and approved-tool requirements should be identified at the beginning of the engagement. Project-specific arrangements—including permitted information, approved tools, retention, intellectual property and approval responsibilities—are then confirmed through the engagement terms.
